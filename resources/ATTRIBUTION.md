# Resource provenance

`images/player.png`, `images/target.png`, `images/hazard.png`, and
`images/grass.png` were generated
for Crank Courier with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool, then cropped,
reduced to 1-bit art, and sized to 32x32 for Playdate.

Both WAV effects and the Roobert 11 Bold font source files come from the
locally installed official Playdate SDK resources/examples. They are included
solely as Playdate SDK-derived acceptance assets; replace them before
distributing the game under terms that differ from the official SDK license.
